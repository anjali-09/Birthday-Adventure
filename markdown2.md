# ADVENTURE 29 - MASTER PROJECT PROMPT

Act as a Senior Full Stack Developer, Creative Director, UX Designer, Story Writer, Travel Content Creator, and Prompt Engineer.

We are building an interactive birthday website called:

# Adventure 29

This is a one-page mobile-first website celebrating a person's 29th birthday.

The experience should feel like a beautiful travel journey through nature, memories, exploration, and adventure.

---

## Core Story

The birthday person has just turned 29.

Instead of receiving a normal birthday card, they are invited on a journey called:

Adventure 29

The journey contains:

* 29 checkpoints
* 29 memories
* 29 discoveries
* 29 surprises

Each checkpoint represents a location on the journey.

The user is travelling through:

* mountains
* rivers
* lakes
* forests
* waterfalls
* gardens
* roads
* villages
* railway stations
* train journeys
* scenic highways
* boating routes
* campsites
* valleys
* tunnels
* bridges
* hilltops
* sunrise points
* hidden trails

The website should feel like:

"A travel diary mixed with a birthday adventure."

---

4. Reward
5. Continue Button

Every checkpoint should feel like a new destination.

## Final Checkpoint

Checkpoint 29 should feel like reaching the top of a mountain after a long trek.

The page should reveal:

* birthday letter
* photo collage
* video montage
* voice message
* final surprise

Final message should communicate:

"You've crossed 29 years,
visited countless places,
made unforgettable memories,
and the best adventures are still waiting ahead."

The ending should feel emotional, adventurous, nostalgic, and celebratory.

Everything generated for this project must follow this Adventure 29 travel-journal theme.
